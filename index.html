Ideal Gas:
$Z = 1$

Van der Waals (VdW):
$Z^3 - (1+B)Z^2 + AZ - AB = 0$
$A = \frac{27}{64} \frac{P_r}{T_r^2}$, $B = \frac{1}{8} \frac{P_r}{T_r}$

Redlich-Kwong (RK):
$Z^3 - Z^2 + (A - B - B^2)Z - AB = 0$
$A = 0.42748 \frac{P_r}{T_r^{2.5}}$, $B = 0.08664 \frac{P_r}{T_r}$

Soave-Redlich-Kwong (SRK):
$Z^3 - Z^2 + (A - B - B^2)Z - AB = 0$
$A = 0.42748 \frac{P_r}{T_r^2} \alpha$, $B = 0.08664 \frac{P_r}{T_r}$
$\alpha = [1 + (0.480 + 1.574\omega - 0.176\omega^2)(1 - \sqrt{T_r})]^2$

Peng-Robinson (PR):
$Z^3 - (1-B)Z^2 + (A - 2B - 3B^2)Z - (AB - B^2 - B^3) = 0$
$A = 0.45724 \frac{P_r}{T_r^2} \alpha$, $B = 0.07780 \frac{P_r}{T_r}$
$\alpha = [1 + (0.37464 + 1.54226\omega - 0.26992\omega^2)(1 - \sqrt{T_r})]^2$

Note: $V = \frac{ZRT}{P}$. If multiple real roots exist, the largest is the vapor root and the smallest is the liquid root.
